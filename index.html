Dinner that survives a toddler.

One-pan lemon chicken orzo. Thirty-five minutes, one pan, and nobody has to wait for the grown-ups' plates to be salted.

Why this one

This is the dinner that gets made when the lamp is already on and someone small is holding my leg. Chicken thighs stay tender while you're distracted, orzo cooks in the same pan as everything else, and the peas go in last so they stay bright.

The lemon goes in off the heat. That's the one step that makes it taste like someone tried, and it takes ten seconds.

One-pan lemon chicken orzo

Prep 10 min · Cook 25 min Serves 4
4

Ingredients

Method

  1. Warm the oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Brown the chicken in one layer for 5 to 6 minutes, turning once. Lift onto a plate.

  2. Lower the heat. Soften the onion in the same pan for 4 minutes. Stir in the garlic and oregano for 30 seconds.

  3. Add the orzo and stir to coat. Pour in the stock, return the chicken and its juices, and bring to a simmer.

  4. Cover and cook for 10 minutes, stirring twice so nothing sticks. Add the peas and cook 2 to 3 minutes more, until the orzo is tender and most of the liquid is absorbed.

  5. Off the heat, stir in the lemon zest, half the juice, the parmesan and the parsley. Taste and add more lemon. Serve the toddler portion first; it cools fastest. Salt the rest at the table.

Notes

Keeps three days in the fridge; loosen with a splash of water when reheating. Freezes well in toddler-sized portions. Tap a step to mark where you are.
