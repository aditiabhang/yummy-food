Banana Maple Chocolate Chip Protein Bread

Oat, almond and coconut flour

  • Gluten-free
  • No refined sugar
  • Butter-free
  • 9 g protein a slice

Made with oat, almond and coconut flour, greek yogurt and eggs. Sweetened with maple syrup.

Made for the 4 o'clock slump: after daycare pickup, or between back-to-back calls. One slice holds a hungry toddler or a tired parent until dinner.

Bakes 55 to 65 minutes. Makes 1 loaf. 9 x 4.5 in loaf pan.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).

  2. Grease or line a 9 × 4.5 in loaf pan.

  3. In a big bowl, mash the bananas. Add the maple syrup, olive oil, eggs, vanilla, yogurt, milk and applesauce. Whisk well.

  4. In another bowl, mix the oat flour, almond flour, coconut powder, protein powder, baking soda and salt.

  5. Pour the dry mix into the wet mix. Stir gently until just mixed.

  6. Stir in the chocolate chips or walnuts, if using.

  7. Pour the batter into the pan. Smooth the top.

  8. Bake for 55 to 65 minutes. The top should be golden. A toothpick in the middle should come out mostly clean.

  9. If the top gets dark too fast, cover it loosely with foil for the last 10 to 15 minutes.

  10. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes. Move it to a rack. Cool it all the way before you slice. This helps it hold together.

Cover with foil if the top browns too fast. Cool all the way before slicing.
